# Official B1 Galerkin Pareto V4 Single-Seed Protocol

V4 is one new prospective, single-root-seed authority. V3.4 is terminal
history and is not modified. The sole scientific repair is to increase the
independent final held-out reference-fit and reference-audit banks from
16,384 to 65,536 samples each. This changes Monte Carlo precision, not the
population Full estimand.

The root is selected outcome-blindly as the first unused value after the prior
authority root within the already declared `20261001..20261099` range. The
result is `20261004`. All stochastic roles are deterministic JAX `fold_in`
descendants of that one root; derived roles are not replicates. No alternate
root may be tested.

The Full estimand is fixed: JAX float64, the frozen permutation-invariant
K=280 dictionary, rank-aware pseudoinverse retaining positive eigenvalues
strictly greater than `1e-12 * lambda_max`, the existing action, and the
existing certificates. Native Galerkin is prohibited and has no fallback.
No infinite-K claim is made.

The requested allowances are exactly 0.5%, 1%, and 2%. Candidate families,
start counts, optimizer, incumbent rules, replacement tolerance, Law
consistency tolerance `1e-4`, and maximum two Law reanchor restarts are the
corrected V3.4 rules. The Law is the first candidate in exact-risk/hash order
that is supported on the original exact roles and passes all four fresh V3.4
guards. Guards use N=8,192, 32,768, 16,384, and 65,536, respectively, with
minimum rESS 0.05 and unchanged projection, forcing, covariance, geometry,
and finite-value gates. The authoritative action audit is reserved from Law
choice. A downstream lower-risk row triggers reanchoring only if its exact
risk improvement exceeds `1e-4` and it passes all four guards.

All selection banks, guards, and held-out roles are fresh V4 roles. Only the
accepted reference, K=280 dictionary, and prospectively declared historical
candidate family may be reused as deterministic inputs after full hash
identity checks. Stochastic statistics and caches from V3.4 or development
are prohibited.

The optimized execution uses JIT/vmap, float64 static shapes, a 512-row direct
Galerkin sufficient-statistic chunk, an 8,192-sample many-body feature chunk,
isolated guard-role processes, and unique-geometry deduplication. It never
materializes a full-bank K-gradient tensor. OOM response is limited to a
scientifically identical smaller static chunk or releasing process state; N,
K, dtype, backend, formulas, thresholds, seed, and winners may not change.

At 0.5%, Law is a mandatory Full baseline. At 1% and 2%, the previous tighter
Full winner is mandatory. Current Tangent is also mandatory for Full. Exact
risk/support feasibility precedes Full action. Full selected train action must
be nonincreasing within `1e-10`.

Final held-out roles may be created only after all seven authoritative rows
pass, `selection/selection_seal.json` exists, and independent reconstruction
of the Law, reanchors, winners, risk constraints, and incumbents passes. The
held-out truth/noise/fit/audit roles are disjoint from selection, prior
authorities, and development by their root/role provenance. Fit and audit N
are frozen at exactly 65,536 each.

The unchanged Full energy statistic at each of 13 time nodes is

```text
abs(a_fit^T K_audit a_fit + a_fit^T f_audit)
-----------------------------------------------------------
max(a_fit^T K_audit a_fit + abs(a_fit^T f_audit), 1e-12)
```

and its certificate is the maximum over time, required to be `<= 0.08`.
Weak `0.12`, gauge `1e-9`, and moment-rate `0.10` thresholds are unchanged.
Every frozen row is evaluated even if an earlier row fails. Any held-out
failure terminates V4 as a preserved failure: no seed switch, bank
regeneration, N/K/threshold change, or post-heldout optimization is allowed.
