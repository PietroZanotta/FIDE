# Official Fast Skyrmion Galerkin Pareto v2 Protocol

This is a prospective, versioned selection-and-validation protocol. Its machine-readable form is `outputs/official_galerkin_pareto_v2/protocol.json`; the SHA-256 sidecar is authoritative. The JSON is frozen before any v2 solver bank, start evaluation, Tangent optimization, or Full optimization.

The first pre-start seal is preserved at `outputs/official_galerkin_pareto_v2_failed_prestart_08dd28ef/`. It terminated after in-memory Stage-A scoring because a periodic-distance helper call omitted its box argument; it wrote no screening result, ran no optimizer, and opened no validation. The replacement seal changes only that call signature, records hashes of the failed attempt, and is frozen before replacement banks are generated. No methodological result from the failed attempt is used.

A second pre-selection seal is preserved at `outputs/official_galerkin_pareto_v2_superseded_galerkin_tesseract_3418fd58/`. The user paused bank rollout before selection to request a separate Galerkin Tesseract. The additive `native/galerkin_tesseract/` CPU/OpenBLAS backend was implemented and passed equivalence tests, but transfer-inclusive RTX 5090 benchmarks made it 3.6–7.3 times slower than direct device-resident JAX. It is therefore retained as an optional CPU fallback and excluded from official decisions. The final seal hashes that implementation, keeps JAX/XLA GPU K/f assembly, and makes partial bank generation resumable.

A third pre-selection seal is preserved at `outputs/official_galerkin_pareto_v2_superseded_native_separation_0890c254/`. It contains no generated bank and no selection result. Candidate-batched projection was moved into the independent `native/candidate_iprojection_tesseract/`, leaving every tracked file in `native/iprojection_tesseract/` identical to `origin/main`. The requested `production_galerkin.assembly_backend` flag is frozen as `jax` for the official run; `tesseract_cpp` remains an opt-in, numerically equivalent K/f assembly backend.

The Full method is the fixed-feature K=280 finite-dimensional Galerkin approximation, using dictionary SHA-256 `37e9b60fcb92c4e5a0ee7ec1651fb7f8889f7ac6bdb02d3bd314e9ef40833326`, rank tolerance `1e-12`, minimum relative ESS `0.05`, maximum held-out energy residual `0.08`, and every existing projection, forcing, covariance, geometry, algebra, weak, gauge, and moment-rate threshold unchanged. Deep Ritz is excluded. The envelope gradient holds coefficients fixed and never differentiates the eigensolve.

Allowances are 0.5, 1, 2, 3, 4, and 5 percent. Selection feasibility is `R_sel <= (1+p/100) R_Law,sel`. Validation feasibility, declared before selection, is `R_val <= (1+p/100+0.05) R_Law,val`; strict nominal-p status and actual risk increase are additionally reported. Law is the frozen config geometry and is defined solely through scientific risk.

New deterministic selection banks have roles/sizes 8192 screening, 32768 search train, 16384 periodic audit, and independent 65536/65536 authoritative train/audit. Their seeds and the fresh validation seeds are derived in the machine protocol under `global_seed:skyrmion:official_pareto_v2:<scope>:<label>`. Validation arrays may not be generated or opened until all Law/Tangent/Full winners and cross-evaluations are sealed.

The deterministic feasible-manifold pool uses Law-to-history interpolation, local perturbations, risk-tangent directions, and a small global component. Stage A never constructs a Galerkin system. Four deterministic starts per method/allowance comprise the mandatory preceding incumbent when available, Law, feasible history, high-rESS, and max-min diverse candidates after deduplication.

Tangent uses the exact four-observable Gram solve. Full uses the K280 search objective. Both use normalized projected-gradient backtracking with initial step `5e-5`, periodic trust radius `2e-4`, at most four accepted-step attempts, at most ten halvings, rank stability, exact scientific gates, and replacement tolerance `1e-10`. Full uses the N=16384 audit at each start, fourth accepted step, and endpoint. Starts need search-side numerical validity but need not pass held-out physical diagnostics. No uncertified endpoint becomes a finalist or incumbent.

At most three deduplicated Full finalists per allowance receive independent N=65536/N=65536 recomputation. Previous certified winners are retained unless a new candidate improves authoritative action by more than `1e-10`. Nested winner actions must be nonincreasing within that tolerance. All Law/Tangent/Full geometries are cross-evaluated using exact risk, Tangent action, and common authoritative K280 Full action before selection is frozen.

Fresh validation sizes are 5000 truth, 16384 reference fit, and 16384 reference audit. This choice is prospective and justified by the completed K280 quadrature qualification: it preserves an independent physical audit while avoiding unnecessary 65536 validation cost. Validation performs no eta optimization and cannot alter selection. Empirical action uncertainty uses the weighted audit-sample standard error and no pseudo-replicates.

Every stage is fail-closed and resumable under protocol, bank, dictionary, dtype, K, eta, allowance, method, config, and code hashes. Exact-geometry hash reuse is permitted only on an identical bank and method. All writes are atomic and isolated beneath the v2 output root.
